Dungeon Cards

Delve 5 dungeon levels. Build your deck. Beat the Lich King.

Goal: defeat the monster on each of 5 levels. Beat the Lich King on level 5 to win.

Your turn: you draw 5 cards and get 3 energy. Click a card (or press 1–9) to play it. Cards cost energy.

Cards: 🗡️ Attack deals damage  ·  🛡️ Block absorbs damage until your next turn  ·  💖 Heal restores HP  ·  ☠️ Poison ticks on the monster after it acts.

Monster: after you press End Turn, the monster attacks. Its next attack is shown above it.

Between levels: pick 1 of 3 new cards for your deck and rest to recover 10 HP.

Keys: 1–9 play cards · Space ends turn · P pauses · M mutes.
